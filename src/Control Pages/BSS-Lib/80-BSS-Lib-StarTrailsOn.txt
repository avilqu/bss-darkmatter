;=== BSS-Lib-StarTrailsOn ===
; Page: BSS-Lib   Button: "Star trails on"   Length: 3.5 s
; Starts star trails (camera exposure on Stars) and hides the Milky Way.
; Trails only grow while time runs: set e.g. Scene DateTime.Rate=600 [3::] after this.
; Split from Theater/Adrien "Star trails"; [3] -> [1:1:1]. Stop with BSS-Lib-StarTrailsOff.
Control Text="Star trails: starting..."
Scene Camera.Exposure.Visibility=0 [0.5]
+0.5
Scene Camera.Exposure.Clear()
Scene Camera.Exposure=On
Scene Camera.Exposure.addAsset(Stars)
Scene Visible Milky Way.Visibility=0 [1:1:1]
Scene Camera.Exposure.Visibility=90 [1:1:1]
+3
Control Text="Star trails on"
