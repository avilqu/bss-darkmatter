;=== BSS-Lib-StarTrailsOff ===
; Page: BSS-Lib   Button: "Star trails off"   Length: 3 s
; Fades out the trails and brings the Milky Way back to the BSS-Lib-SkyBright level (80).
; Does not stop time: do that in the calling script.
; Split from Theater/Adrien "Star trails". The original also ran
; Control ExpSun.Reset() and Control camExpButton.Reset() (buttons on another page):
; left out here.
Control Text="Star trails: stopping..."
Scene Camera.Exposure.Visibility=0 [1:1:1]
Scene Visible Milky Way.Visibility=80 [1:1:1]
+3
Scene Camera.Exposure.removeAsset(Stars)
Scene Camera.Exposure.Clear()
Scene Camera.Exposure=Off
Control Text="Star trails off"
